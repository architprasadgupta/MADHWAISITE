The problem

India runs on street food. The system was never built for the people making it.

A vendor can sell to hundreds of customers a day and still be invisible to a bank, a landlord, or a government scheme. MadhWai closes three gaps at once.

No legal identity

Without a Certificate of Vending, there is no proof of livelihood — so no loans, no insurance, no protection from eviction.

No payment record

Cash in, cash out. Nothing a lender can read, and no cushion when a slow week or a health scare hits.

No route to customers

Aggregators favour restaurants and dark kitchens. The stall two lanes away never shows up in the app.

Sustainability

The oil doesn't go down the drain.

On every delivery run, riders collect used cooking oil from vendors and carry it back for recycling into biodiesel. Vendors earn a small margin; the neighbourhood keeps its water clean.

Paired with compostable packaging, it turns each order into a slightly smaller footprint rather than a larger one.
